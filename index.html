Every batch of produce deserves a next step.

When a buyer falls through, a truck is late or prices drop, good produce can lose its value within days. ANNA CYCLE helps farmers, vendors, buyers and storage operators decide what happens next, before the loss.

Who benefits

Farmers and vendors

Reach more buyers than one market day allows, sell a big batch in parts, and find storage or another route when plans change.

Buyers

See nearby produce with its reported condition and collection deadline, and reserve only the quantity needed.

The local food system

Surplus stays in local circulation. Short-term storage and shared transport use what a community already has.

One platform, four roles

    Try a split sale

    A seller has 100 kg of tomatoes. Move the sliders to reserve kilograms for three buyers. Try asking for more than is left.

    This runs in your browser as a demonstration. In the app, the same check runs on the server so two buyers can’t claim the same kilograms.

    Honest by design

    • Recommendations are rule-based. There is no AI model behind them.
    • No shelf-life predictions. We record reported condition, times and handling.
    • Food safety checks come before financial recovery.
    • Storage stays one to three days at most, and never fits every crop.
    • Demonstration hubs and listings are labelled as sample data.
    • We don’t claim to end food waste. We aim to reduce avoidable loss.

    Impact is counted in separate steps: listed, matched, sold, collected, redirected, and verified. A listing is not saved food until its outcome is verified, and estimated value is always labelled as an estimate.
